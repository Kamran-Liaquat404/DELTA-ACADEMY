About DELTA Academy

Building strong academic foundations and practical skills in Balochistan

DELTA English Language & Computer Academy was established with the vision of providing quality, affordable, and practical education to the students of Kharan and surrounding areas.

The academy is committed to developing students' English communication skills, computer literacy, academic excellence, and personal confidence.

Our goal is to prepare learners for school, college, competitive examinations, higher education, and professional careers through modern teaching methods and a supportive learning environment.
